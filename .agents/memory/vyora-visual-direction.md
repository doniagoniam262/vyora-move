---
name: VYORA visual direction
description: User's visual fidelity and inclusive-audience requirements.
---
VYORA is one product for women and men. The onboarding choice controls a pink theme for female profiles and a blue theme for male profiles.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested separate pink/female and dark-blue/male experiences and no opposite-gender figures in the selected profile.

**How to apply:** Keep one VYORA brand and shared goal-based plans. If athlete photography is used, filter it to the selected profile and never infer gender from account data.

Keep polished gradient fitness cards for browsing. The movement guide must not show cartoon human figures; use non-figurative icons, written technique, and the exercise's existing photos or video.

**Why:** The user clarified that cartoon figures should be removed for both women and men, while the exercises themselves remain.

**How to apply:** Remove human-figure animation from exercise guidance without deleting catalog exercises, photos, videos, or written instructions.

Never embed or link YouTube videos. Keep movement education fully inside VYORA, with primary and secondary muscle targets, exercise steps, breathing, common errors, and easier variations in Arabic and English.

**Why:** The user explicitly repeated that YouTube must be excluded and asked for in-app movements that identify each target muscle.

**How to apply:** Keep the muscle-target filter and localized coaching cues. Explain benefits and technique for every target muscle in readable cards alongside internal movement animation. Do not add external video players, YouTube links, or the removed exercise-photo carousel.

Differentiate VYORA by recommending workouts around real home constraints such as available time, limited space, and quiet/no-jump needs.

**Why:** The user approved making home-specific training conditions the product's point of difference.

**How to apply:** Prefer practical, locally stored home-workout adaptations over generic gym-app features.

When users select weight gain as a goal, provide a goal-scoped resistance plan and general meal guidance that reflects their selected weekly frequency; avoid medical calorie prescriptions or guaranteed results.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for a distinct weight-gain option with a tailored training and nutrition plan.

**How to apply:** Keep the plan gradual, include recovery and a clear nutrition disclaimer, and show it only for the matching profile goal.

Nutrition ideas should adapt to the selected goal but remain general, culturally practical, and non-medical. Adult maintenance-energy estimates may use explicitly entered age, activity, measurements and gender; body shape must not determine calories or imply targeted fat loss.

**Why:** The user requested a goal-based weekly food section and later a profile calorie calculator. A maintenance estimate is educational, not a personalized diet prescription.

**How to apply:** Keep meal examples tied to the goal. Do not invent missing calculator inputs, impose calorie restrictions, or promise outcomes. State the adult-only estimate's limits and recommend qualified advice for individual needs.

The five-shape body guide uses the user's supplied female reference photos and is shown only to female profiles.

**Why:** The user requested a visual body-shape guide for women while repeatedly requiring that the selected gender profile never show opposite-gender imagery.

**How to apply:** Reuse uploaded assets rather than generating replacements, and gate both the More-page link and the route content by profile gender.

Do not simulate paid access or a successful checkout. The monthly subscription page stays informational until a payment provider is connected and configured.

**Why:** The user dismissed the provider-connection choice and asked to continue without an integration.

**How to apply:** Keep checkout disabled, do not invent a price or active subscription state, and only enable payment after a real provider flow is configured.

Bug fixes must preserve saved user data and all existing app sections.

**Why:** The user explicitly repeated that repairing a crash must not lose any data or sections already added.

**How to apply:** Do not clear browser storage or reset profiles to address rendering failures. Preserve backward compatibility and verify saved-profile round trips. For motion bugs, test timing boundaries as well as initial rendering: a valid first image does not prove later frame indices are safe.

The exercise catalog, titles, instructions, and plan IDs are shared across female and male profiles; profile gender changes the selected visual assets, not the exercise list.

**Why:** The user confirmed that the same set of 16 exercises and names applies to both groups while imagery is matched by gender.

**How to apply:** Keep one database-backed catalog for both profiles. Do not fork exercise records by gender; choose gender-specific media at presentation time.

When a video exists only for one gender, filter that exercise only from the other gender's video-led experience; preserve the shared catalog and the matching-gender experience.

**Why:** The user clarified that removing exercises without boys' videos must not remove those exercises from the girls' catalog.

**How to apply:** Keep database records shared, and apply gender-specific video availability in library, workout-plan, and session views.
