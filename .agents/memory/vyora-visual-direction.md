VYORA is one product for women and men, but users choose their gender in onboarding. After selection, use a pink theme and women-only athlete imagery for female profiles, or a dark-blue theme and men-only athlete imagery for male profiles.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested separate pink/female and dark-blue/male experiences and no opposite-gender figures in the selected profile.

**How to apply:** Keep one VYORA brand and shared fitness plans, but filter every visible athlete image and video presenter to the selected profile. Never infer gender from account data.

Use the user's supplied reference photos that match the selected profile, and closely reproduce the requested visual direction rather than inventing a loosely inspired alternative. Avoid mixed-gender photos after profile selection.

Build movement education as an in-app guide, not a list of generic YouTube videos. Explain primary and secondary muscles, movement cues, breathing, common errors, and safer/easier variations in Arabic and English.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected generic YouTube workouts and asked for professional, muscle-specific movement details inside VYORA.

**How to apply:** Keep guidance self-contained and gender-safe. Do not reintroduce the home exercise-photo carousel after it has been removed; use movement diagrams for anatomy guidance.

Differentiate VYORA by recommending workouts around real home constraints such as available time, limited space, and quiet/no-jump needs.

**Why:** The user approved making home-specific training conditions the product's point of difference.

**How to apply:** Prefer practical, locally stored home-workout adaptations over generic gym-app features.

When users select weight gain as a goal, provide a goal-scoped resistance plan and general meal guidance that reflects their selected weekly frequency; avoid medical calorie prescriptions or guaranteed results.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for a distinct weight-gain option with a tailored training and nutrition plan.

**How to apply:** Keep the plan gradual, include recovery and a clear nutrition disclaimer, and show it only for the matching profile goal.
