---
name: VYORA visual direction
description: User's visual fidelity and inclusive-audience requirements.
---
VYORA is one product for women and men. The onboarding choice controls a pink theme for female profiles and a blue theme for male profiles; exercise illustrations should remain gender-neutral, with no opposite-gender-specific content.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested separate pink/female and dark-blue/male experiences and no opposite-gender figures in the selected profile.

**How to apply:** Keep one VYORA brand and shared goal-based plans. If athlete photography is used, filter it to the selected profile and never infer gender from account data.

Prefer distinct custom vector movement illustrations over repeated workout photography or a home exercise carousel. Supplied brand references may still inform VYORA identity; do not show an opposite-gender athlete after profile selection.

**Why:** The user later called the current workout photos traditional and repetitive and asked for modern vector or 3D illustrations instead.

**How to apply:** Use varied, accessible movement illustrations that inherit the profile accent color, while preserving real interactive pages, the timer, and locally saved progress.

Build movement education as an in-app guide, not a list of generic YouTube videos. Explain primary and secondary muscles, movement cues, breathing, common errors, and safer/easier variations in Arabic and English.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected generic YouTube workouts and asked for professional, muscle-specific movement details inside VYORA.

**How to apply:** Keep guidance self-contained and gender-safe. Do not reintroduce the home exercise-photo carousel after it has been removed; use movement diagrams for anatomy guidance.

Differentiate VYORA by recommending workouts around real home constraints such as available time, limited space, and quiet/no-jump needs.

**Why:** The user approved making home-specific training conditions the product's point of difference.

**How to apply:** Prefer practical, locally stored home-workout adaptations over generic gym-app features.

When users select weight gain as a goal, provide a goal-scoped resistance plan and general meal guidance that reflects their selected weekly frequency; avoid medical calorie prescriptions or guaranteed results.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for a distinct weight-gain option with a tailored training and nutrition plan.

**How to apply:** Keep the plan gradual, include recovery and a clear nutrition disclaimer, and show it only for the matching profile goal.

Nutrition ideas should adapt to the selected goal but remain general, culturally practical, and non-medical; do not promise outcomes or prescribe calories.

**Why:** The user requested a dedicated weekly food section tailored to weight loss, muscle building, general fitness, and weight gain.

**How to apply:** Keep daily meal examples and simple tips tied to the profile goal, with a clear note to consult a qualified professional for individual needs.

Do not simulate paid access or a successful checkout. The monthly subscription page stays informational until a payment provider is connected and configured.

**Why:** The user dismissed the provider-connection choice and asked to continue without an integration.

**How to apply:** Keep checkout disabled, do not invent a price or active subscription state, and only enable payment after a real provider flow is configured.
